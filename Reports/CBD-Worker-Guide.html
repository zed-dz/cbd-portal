CBD Plant & Labour

Worker Guide

Everything you need to use the worker portal on your phone. Your portal: cbd-portal-gray.vercel.app
Tip: open it in your phone browser, then tap Share → Add to Home Screen so it opens like an app. There's also a 💬 Portal Help button (bottom-right) inside the app — tap it and ask anything in plain English.

1. How do I get into the app? (first time)

The office has already created your worker profile. To get your own login:

1 Open cbd-portal-gray.vercel.app on your phone.
2 Tap Create account.
3 Use the same email address the office has on file for you (ask if you're not sure — a different email won't find your profile).
4 Choose a password, then tap Create account. You're in.

If you ever see "No worker profile found", you signed up with an email the office doesn't have — just tell the office which email you used. Already have a login? Tap Sign in.

2. How do I see where I'm going? (job placements)

Tap the 📋 My Allocations tab. Each job shows:

  • Site and Client — who you're working for
  • Project and Address — where to go
  • Site Supervisor + their phone — who to ask for on site
  • Date and Start time — when, plus any notes on what to bring or do

This updates whenever the office books you on a new job. (SMS text alerts are coming soon.)

3. How do timesheets work? How do I submit one?

You have two easy ways — use whichever suits the day:

A. Clock In / Out (simplest) — tap ⏱ Clock In/Out:
  • Tap ▶ Clock In when you start. The timer runs.
  • Tap ■ Clock Out when you finish — your hours are logged automatically.
B. Submit a timesheet (full control) — tap 🕐 My Timesheets → + Submit Timesheet:
  • Shift Type: Standard Shift, or Rain Day if you were rained off
  • Date, Client, Site, Hours, and Notes
  • Use Notes for anything that affects pay — e.g. "left early 2pm", "worked through lunch", "no smoko"
  • Tap Submit

Every timesheet starts as Pending until the office approves it. The coloured boxes at the top show your total pending / approved / rejected hours. Submit daily so your pay is right.

4. Can I access timesheets on my phone? Yes.

The whole portal is built for phones. The 🕐 My Timesheets tab shows every timesheet you've submitted and its status (Pending / Approved / Rejected) plus your hours totals. You can submit a new one any time, anywhere — on site, in the ute, or at home.

5. How do I keep my details up to date?

Tap 👤 My Profile to update your:

  • Mobile and address — when you move house or change your number
  • Tickets / licences — White Card, HR licence, EWP, etc., as you earn them
  • Driver licence and emergency contact

Tap Save profile. Doing this keeps you eligible for the right jobs and reachable in an emergency.

Bank, super and tax details are handled separately by the office for your privacy — you won't see those here.

Other tabs

📜 My Certifications — your tickets/cards on file and their expiry dates. If one is going red (expiring), renew it and tell the office.

Quick answers

How am I notified of a new job?
Today: check My Allocations. Soon: a text message the moment you're booked.
Forgot your password?
Use the reset link on the Sign in screen, or ask the office to re-invite you.
Wrong email on file?
The office updates your email on the Workers page; then use Create account again with the correct one.
Stuck inside the app?
Tap the 💬 Portal Help bubble (bottom-right) and ask your question in plain English.
